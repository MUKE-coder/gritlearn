Why analogies stick
By Jane Doe · March 12, 2026

I used to think analogies were a soft skill — a nice-to-have for explaining things to friends at parties. Then I started teaching code, and I realized analogies are the entire game.

When I explain closures with words alone, students nod. When I say "closures are a backpack the function carries with it," they laugh, and a week later they still remember.

The mechanism

An analogy gives an abstract idea a physical handle.

Where they go wrong

An analogy can also overpromise. Pick the analogy carefully — and tell them where it leaks.
