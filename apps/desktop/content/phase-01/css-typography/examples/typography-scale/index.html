Heading 1 — --text-4xl at 1.2 leading

Body text at --text-base. Notice the breathing room from line-height: 1.6. Long-form reading needs this generous a measure or the eye loses its place between lines.

Heading 2 — --text-2xl

The 1.25× ratio between scale steps gives each level a clear "I'm one rank higher than the previous" feel. Two adjacent steps look different; non-adjacent steps look very different.

Heading 3 — --text-xl

By this point the scale is doing the heavy lifting. You're not picking fonts, you're picking a slot on the scale.

Heading 4 — --text-lg

Small headings still use the same family, just a smaller slot.
